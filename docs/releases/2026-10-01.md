# 2026-10-01

Release version: `2026.10.1`

## Changes

- `dev-std plan close` now accepts fully completed active or pending plans.
- Completed pending plans no longer require a direct frontmatter status edit
  before closeout.
- Blocked plans remain ineligible for deletion until their status is resolved
  or deliberately returned to pending.
- The TypeSpec reference template now overrides transitive `undici` usage to
  7.30.0, clearing the high-severity advisories found during release signoff.

## Validation

- An end-to-end regression test completes the final step of a pending plan and
  closes it through the public CLI.
- A focused regression test proves that a completed blocked plan is retained.
- Full Rack signoff, package build, Twine validation, and installed-wheel
  validation are required before publication.
